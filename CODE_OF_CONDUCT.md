# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes when they occur.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, intimidation, unwelcome sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using [GitHub's contact and reporting tools](https://github.com/contact) for the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and promptly as possible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.
